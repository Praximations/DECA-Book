# Visual ideas for the presentation (15 minutes, hand-carried, no power or internet supplied)

These reuse the book's design system so everything reads as one brand. Each can be generated from this repo.

## Leave-behinds (allowed: flyers, brochures, business cards)
1. **One-page "Loyalty Plan at a Glance"**: the strategy, the 3–4 KPIs, the budget and the ROI on one 8.5 × 11 sheet. Judges keep it while they score.
2. **Mock loyalty card / punch card / membership card**: printed on card stock, showing the proposed program's name and reward tiers.
3. **Consultant business cards** for each participant, matching the team's role as hired consultants.
4. **Tri-fold brochure** the business could hand to its own customers to launch the program.

## Things to show during the talk (must be battery-powered or printed)
5. **Customer journey map**: first visit → second visit → regular → advocate, marking where the plan steps in at each stage.
6. **Before / after KPI dashboard**: current repeat rate against the target, as a printed poster or on a tablet.
7. **Persona cards**: two or three customer personas drawn from the survey's psychographics, each with a photo, needs and what brings them back.
8. **App or text-message mockups**: phone-frame mockups of a rewards app, a birthday message or a "we miss you" win-back text, shown on a tablet or printed.
9. **12-month rollout timeline**: a foldable poster or tabletop strip.
10. **ROI waterfall**: investment, then incremental revenue, then net return, drawn as one big, simple chart.
11. **Survey highlight board**: the three most surprising statistics, set as huge numbers.
12. **Physical prototype**: a branded tabletop tent card, QR sign-up stand or reward token the business would actually use.

## Delivery tips that score under "Delivery" and "Presentation Design"
- Hand the judge the leave-behind at the moment you reach the budget, so they hold the numbers while you explain them.
- Have each participant own one section of the visuals (every participant must present).
- Rehearse the setup. Setup time counts against the 15 minutes.
